## Reference (from EXPERIENCE §4.7)

- **Materials:** walnut, brass, frostedGlass, ceramic, ceramicGraphite, accentGlow.
- **Motion (one):** the cup and lid rock in the gimbal: roll = 9° sin(2πt/9), pitch = 6° sin(4πt/9 + π/4). The tamper ring brightens from 0.2 to 1.6 while abs(roll) > 8° (a consequence of the same motion). Period 9 s.
- **Reduced-motion pose:** rolled 10°, tamper ring lit at 1.2 (the story moment).
- **Object description (DOM):** "A cup rocks gently inside a brass gimbal. Its cut-away lid shows a Raspberry Pi Pico W and an orientation sensor, and the ring on the lid lights when the tilt crosses the tamper threshold."

2. Board and chip use `ceramicGraphite`, the 20 instanced pads `brass`; no green anywhere. Board 0.06 x 0.004 x 0.024 (Pico W proportions), ten pads per long edge at 6 mm pitch wrapping the edge; unit-tested in `tamper.test.ts`.
3. The lid is a hard-edged Lathe with ten 3 mm risers (`layerRadius`, quadratic shoulder from r 0.100 to 0.090), and the cut faces are closed with the same stepped section; the lid material is the shared `ceramic` with no map and no shader change.
4. One clock (`useExhibitClock`): roll 9 degrees sin(2 pi t / 9), pitch 6 degrees sin(4 pi t / 9 + pi / 4); the ring glow is a pure function of the same phase, 0.2 whenever |roll| <= 8 degrees (unit-tested at 5 ms steps over a period), up to 1.6 with 0.6 s ramps. Manifest `motion: 'slow'`. Probe with `&idle=1`: the canvas changes at 2, 4 and 6 s with motion on.
5. No Text, Html, texture or text geometry in the component; no liquid in the cup, no hand or figure; the shots show none.
6. Exactly three arcs (`GIZMO_AXES` roll, pitch, yaw), one InstancedMesh written once in a layout effect, outside the rocking groups; their plane normals are Z, X and Y (unit-tested). Radius 0.20 instead of 0.16 so the rocking lid never crosses them (at least 5 mm clearance over the cycle, unit-tested); noted as a deviation in the README.
7. `stats().exhibits['safe-sip']` on desktop and pixel7: `inBounds: true`, 5,620 triangles (budget 9,000, limit 20,000), 12 draw calls; the unit test also checks every vertex inside the standard slot over the whole cycle. Materials: walnut, brass, frostedGlass, ceramic, ceramicGraphite and one `useMaterialInstance('accentGlow')`; nothing else.
