# WP-11: Environment v1

## Goal
The greybox becomes the gallery: warm baked light pools and contact shadows in a floor lightmap, a reflective floor on high tier and an environment-mapped floor elsewhere, real spotlights fixed per tier (3 high, 1 medium and low) that follow the visitor, a Lightformer environment, fog, beam cones, the lobby with its arch, "COMMIT HISTORY" letters and the duotone portrait, the year strips, door reveals and room names, the Foundations sign, and the front desk with its four objects. The Art Director picks ACES or AgX from side-by-side screenshots and freezes it as a token. Every stop stays inside its render budgets on every viewport.

## Owner role
R3 Environment Builder, with R2 Art Director for the tone-mapping decision and light values.

## Milestone and dependencies
M2. Must be merged first: WP-06 to WP-10 (gate G1). Parallel with: WP-12 to WP-16, WP-18. Feeds: WP-17 (slice tests), WP-19 (review), G2.

## Forbidden
- Standard list (`docs/wp/_TEMPLATE.md`).
- Changing the light count or material types at runtime (tier is fixed at boot; only DPR adapts), shadow maps, transmission, post-processing, HDRI downloads, GLB models.
- Readable text only in 3D: every 3D label duplicates DOM text (EXPERIENCE §5.4 list only).
- New colors or materials outside the library without R2's entry in ART_DIRECTION.md.
- Committing screenshots (they go to `out/wp11/`).

## Notes
- Changing light count or material type at runtime recompiles every shader; that is why tiers are fixed at boot and only DPR adapts.
- Count the reflector pass: `StatsProbe` sets `gl.info.autoReset = false`, so high-tier draw calls include it; the 150 limit is for high only.
- Troika glyph textures and CanvasTextures can come back blank after a context restore; WP-16 remounts the canvas instead of trusting three's in-place restore. Keep every generated texture reproducible from code.
- No real-time shadows: plinth shadows are painted into the lightmap and a decal; contact shadows render once.
- Far exhibits still glow because every target keeps its baked pool and wall wash even without a real light.
- Under `?test=1` everything is deterministic (seeded textures, frozen clock), so CI screenshots are stable.
