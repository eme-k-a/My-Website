# WP-27: Mobile and onboarding

## Goal
The phone experience is comfortable and complete: stop cards and the HUD never collide, cards never cover the case, every control is within thumb reach and at least 44 px, safe areas are respected, landscape phones get the compact desktop layout, and the onboarding touches (admission ticket stamp and tear, reduced-motion chip, hints) land without layout shift.

## Owner role
R6 UI & Classic Engineer.

## Milestone and dependencies
M4, after gate G3. Must be merged first: WP-20 to WP-25. Parallel with: WP-26, WP-28. Feeds: G4.

## Forbidden
- Standard list (`docs/wp/_TEMPLATE.md`).
- Drag-to-rotate, swipe-to-close, a multi-height sheet, a floor-plan modal, custom gestures, `touch-action: none`, disabling page zoom.
- Hover-only information.
