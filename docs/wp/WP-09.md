# WP-09: Scroll track + rail

## Goal
Walking the gallery is native page scrolling: one snapping section per stop, a camera that follows `scrollY` through measured section offsets with maath damping along a Catmull-Rom rail, wing tracks entered with "Step inside" or Enter at a door and left with Esc or Back, and a working `window.__MUSEUM__` test hook (`goTo`, `screenPos`, `stop`, `settled`). Trackpad momentum, mouse notches, keyboard and phone flicks all land on whole stops without skipping, and nothing hijacks input.

## Forbidden
- Standard list (`docs/wp/_TEMPLATE.md`).
- Any `wheel`, `mousewheel`, `touchstart`, `touchmove` or `touchend` listener, `onWheel` or `onTouch*` prop, `preventDefault` on scroll input, `touch-action: none`, drei `ScrollControls`, or JS scroll snapping.
- Custom keys other than Enter and Esc (arrows, Space, PageUp/PageDown, Home, End stay native).
- Writing the URL or history on stop arrival (scroll, snap, keys, HUD). History is written only by story open/step/close and wing enter/exit.
- `section.offsetTop` estimates such as `i * innerHeight`.
- Changing the frozen contract types (request through R0).

## Notes
- macOS trackpads and free-spin wheels keep sending wheel events for 1 to 2 s after release; any JS "one gesture = one stop" logic misfires. Native scroll plus `scroll-snap-stop: always` on every section is the fix; the 40-event decaying stream test proves it.
- `touch-action: none` on iOS stops the address bar from collapsing and feels like a trap. Never use it.
- Safari rate-limits history writes (about 100 calls in 30 s): writing the URL on every arrival would throw during fast scrolling. Only story and wing actions write history.
- Measure real `offsetTop`s (cards and fonts change section heights). Re-measure after `document.fonts.ready` and on ResizeObserver callbacks.
- Sections are `pointer-events: none` so empty-area clicks fall through to `#app`, where R3F raycasts them; cards opt back in with `[data-card]`.
- Exiting a wing overrides the browser's scroll restoration with `mainScrollY` in a layout effect after the main sections re-render.
- `screenPos` must return client CSS px of the hit-box center so `page.mouse.click(x, y)` hits the case; WP-07's exhibit contract spec depends on it.

- R0 ruling (2026-10-15): add one keydown handler (ArrowUp, ArrowDown, PageUp, PageDown, Space, Home, End) that calls `scrollIntoView` on the adjacent section; ignore it when focus is in a form field or the dialog is open. Scrolling stays native; wheel and touch listeners remain banned. Acceptance: ArrowDown twice from the lobby lands on `projects-door`, and three times lands on `mysa`.
