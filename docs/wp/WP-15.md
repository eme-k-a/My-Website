# WP-15: Camera feel

## Goal
Walking feels considered: the camera looks down the aisle while moving and turns toward each case as it arrives, frames the case right of the desktop card column and above the phone card, adds a whisper of pointer parallax on desktop, dollies in when a story opens, and under reduced motion cuts between stops with a 200 ms fade and no in-between positions. The onboarding hint appears and hides on the right cues, and the live region announces arrivals only when the keyboard or the HUD caused them.

## Notes
- Announcing every arrival during scrolling spams screen readers; only keyboard and HUD moves announce.
- Framing by `setViewOffset` keeps the pinned layout coordinates valid on every aspect; never move stop poses per device.
- Damping uses delta time (`Math.min(delta, 0.1)`), which stays correct when phones throttle to 30 fps.
- Reduced motion must not sample in-between poses even during a long scroll; hold the last stop until the scroll arrives.
- Parallax uses a passive `pointermove`; it is not an input model and never moves the camera along the rail.
