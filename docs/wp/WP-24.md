# WP-24: Wing rooms and doors

## Goal
The two side wings are finished rooms rather than greybox boxes: walls, ceiling, floor pools, plinths, wall washes and door signage derived from each door, with door openings and thresholds that read as invitations from the aisle. A clearance test proves the camera never comes within 0.4 m of a wall on any rail, and that moving a wing in content moves its whole room.

## Notes
- Linear wing tracks with "turn to face" stops avoid trap states; there is no branching rail and no automatic exit at the end of a wing.
- The door stop pose is index 0 of the wing track, shared with the main track, so entering a wing has no jump.
- The midpoint rule keeps every door 6 m from the nearest plinth on either wall, so either side is collision-free; the moved-wing test proves it.
