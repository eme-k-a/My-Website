# F-038: the wing HUD covers the door title when a door stop is reached inside its wing

## Goal
Close P1 F-038 from G2 review round 3 (`review/runs/2026-10-20-g2r3/scorecard.md`, `review/findings.json`). A visitor who presses Step inside at a door and then scrolls or swipes back up once lands on the door section on the wing track (EXPERIENCE §3.4 step 2). The camera returns to the door pose, but the HUD keeps the wing head. On Pixel 7 the "Return to the aisle" button (x 16 to 189, y 96 to 140) covers VOLUNTEER and the PRO of PROJECTS on the door transom titles. On desktop the wing chip clips the last P of LEADERSHIP. The probe shots are `probe-*-door-in-wing-*.png` in that folder.

## Owner role
R6 Overlay, with R4 if framing changes. There is no context pack: read this brief, the round 3 scorecard and finding, EXPERIENCE §3.4, and the end of `docs/RULINGS.md`.

## Fix
At a door stop on either track, no HUD element may intersect the projected bounds of the door transom title, on desktop and Pixel 7. That covers the wing chip, Return to the aisle and the rail label. Choose the simplest of:
- hide or lower the wing head while the current stop is the door on the wing track; Esc and the door card still offer every exit;
- show the main-track HUD at the door section.

Keep keyboard and screen-reader access to every exit control that stays visible. A control that hides must not trap focus.

## Acceptance
Extend `tests/e2e/rail-input.spec.ts` (g) so that at each door it steps inside the wing, presses ArrowUp once and waits for `settled`. Every visible rail and HUD rect must then avoid the projected transom title, on desktop and pixel7. With the fix disabled the new check must fail. `npm run verify`, `npm run build`, and the rail-input, wings, keyboard and dialog specs pass on desktop and pixel7, and a11y stays at 0 violations. Re-take the two probe captures and look at them.
