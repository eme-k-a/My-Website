# WP-21: Builder B: CFIA, MUN, Certifications

## Goal
Exhibits No. 04 "The Ticket Spindle" (CFIA), No. 05 "Work in Progress" (MUN, on the large plinth) and No. 06 "The Medal Cabinet" (certifications, in the wall vitrine) replace their placeholders. Each is one object with one slow motion, from shared materials, inside its slot variant, under 20k triangles, in its own lazy chunk.

- Standard list (`docs/wp/_TEMPLATE.md`); everything ESLint bans in exhibit folders; cross-exhibit imports.
- CFIA: a growing stack, a counter, "RESOLVED" or any text, a CRT monitor, a pin-light ring, figures.
- MUN: a crest, logo, text, percentage, number or progress bar.
- Certifications: issuer logos, official badge images, text; any material other than brass (the walnut back panel is the kit's).
- A second motion; more than 20,000 triangles each; leaving the slot variant.

## Notes
- CFIA gets the warmest light in the building (age parameter 1); keep paper and walnut values from the library so the warmth reads from the light, not from new colors.
- The visible ticket count is constant: the stack sinks as each ticket lands. No counts anywhere.
- The certifications vitrine is wall-mounted: local +Z points out of the wall toward the visitor; check the frame's rotation with the kit before modeling.
- Use instancing for pins, traces, rods and tickets; triangle estimates are about 1,000 (CFIA), 2,100 (MUN), 3,700 (certifications).
- Issue dates and issuers are placeholders in content; nothing in 3D depends on them.
