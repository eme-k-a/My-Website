## Evidence (one line per box, same order)

Build on `claude/m3-wp-21` (WP-21), `?test=1&mode=museum`, captured with the `@capture` spec into `out/wp21/` (scratch, regenerated by the WP-21 commands). Motion probe: built site, `?test=1&mode=museum&idle=1`, canvas shots at 0, 3 and 8 s after settling, with and without `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`.

1. Thumbnail `out/wp21/certifications-thumb.png`: one brass rail with three discs hanging in a row inside the vitrine on the walnut panel; one object.
2. The component draws only brass primitives (cylinders, frustums, pyramids, a torus, boxes); no Text, Html, texture or image. The emblems are generic shapes (a cloud, a four-point compass rose, a three-blade turbine), none an issuer mark or badge.
3. Case crop thumbnail `out/wp21/certifications-crop-thumb.png` (EXPERIENCE §4 "a 320 px wide grayscale crop of the stop screenshot"): a cloud of three lobes on a bar, a four-point star and a three-blade rotor are each distinct. In the full-frame thumbnail the medallions are about 10 px wide, so emblems need the crop; their bevelled flanks are what make them read (a flat brass top mirrors the dark room).
4. One clock-driven motion: `medallionYaw` +-12 degrees, period 12 s, each 4 s after its neighbour, easing in from rest. Probe: with `&idle=1` the canvas changes; under reduced motion it is identical at 0, 3 and 8 s. Manifest `motion: 'slow'`.
5. `stats().exhibits.certifications` on desktop and pixel7: `inBounds: true` against the wall vitrine slot, 3,924 triangles (budget 5,000), 7 draw calls; `cabinet.test.ts` checks every vertex inside the slot at rest and at full swing. `MedalCabinet.tsx` uses `useMaterial('brass')` only.
6. The 3D object depends on no date or issuer. The content (`src/content/resume.ts`) is the only source for names, dates and issuers; the certifications exhibit, story and object copy are `draft: true`, so `RELEASE=1 npm run check:copy` fails on them. Note: the content currently omits the unknown dates and the Tech Stewardship issuer rather than printing a `[placeholder: ...]` (CONTENT_DRAFT §7 item 8); that is R1's call, recorded for R0 in the WP-21 report.
