- **Zero shadow maps** (`gl.shadowMap.enabled = false`, no `castShadow`). Contact shadows render once (§7.4).
- **Environment** from drei `<Environment frames={1} resolution={256}>` with `<Lightformer>`s. No HDR or HDRI download.

## From EXPERIENCE §6.9

### 6.9 Time in the space

**Brass year strips** (the age map):
- Time anchors along the main aisle: each main work exhibit at `(z_i, mid_i)`, where `mid_i` is the middle of its date range as a half-open month interval, `mid_i = (ym(start) + ym(end) + 1) / 2` with `ym = year * 12 + (month - 1)`; plus the aisle end at `(zLast - 6, ym(start of the last exhibit))`. Anchor times must decrease with z; the layout clamps each to at least one month below the previous.
- The current build year's strip sits at the arch (z -4). For each earlier year Y down to the oldest start year, the strip labeled Y sits where the anchors' linear interpolation reaches January of Y + 1 (the point where, walking on, you enter year Y). A boundary newer than the first exhibit's midpoint is placed at `z_0 + 3`.
- Geometry: brass inlay 0.03 m wide across x -3.3 to 3.3, flush with the floor. Numerals lie flat at x 0, z = strip z - 0.45, reading in the walking direction.

| Strip | z today | Where it reads |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | -4 | At the arch |
| 2025 | -25.5 | Between Mysa and the volunteer door |
| 2024 | -37.5 | Just past Wailo |
| 2023 | -44.5 | Just before CFIA |
| 2022 | -51.4 | Just past CFIA, at the end of the aisle |

Spotlight, pool and wall-wash color sample a ramp through `light-0` to `light-3` at t = 0, 1/3, 2/3, 1 (linear RGB interpolation), so today's four exhibits get exactly the four tokens. A wing uses its door's position: `t = (k + 0.5) / (n - 1)` for `after = w[k]`. Lobby and portrait use `light-0`; Foundations uses `light-3`; the desk uses `light-1`.

## From EXPERIENCE §7.5

### 7.5 Lighting recipe (fixed per tier at boot)
- **Ambient:** hemisphere light, sky `#1A1C22`, ground `#0B0B0D`, intensity 0.15.
- **Real spotlights:** 3 on high, 1 on medium and low (§6.8). Angle 0.38 rad, penumbra 0.6, decay 2, `castShadow` false. Starting intensity 40 cd, tuned by R2 in WP-11 and frozen as a token.
- **Zero shadow maps.**
- **Baked light pools:** `lightPools.ts` paints a CanvasTexture lightmap for each floor zone. Per target: a radial pool of radius 1.6 m, centered 0.3 m from the plinth toward the aisle center, peak alpha 0.85 in its temperature color, smoothstep falloff. Door thresholds get a smaller pool (radius 1.2 m, alpha 0.35, `light-0`) on the aisle floor so doors invite.
- **Contact shadows, rendered once:** painted into the same lightmap (plinth footprint, soft edge 0.25 m, darkness 0.55), plus the plinth-top contact shadow (§5.2).
- **Wall wash:** a shared 256 px "scallop" texture on wall bays behind each target, tinted by its temperature; panel seams every 2 m are baked into it.
- **Environment:** drei `Environment` with `frames={1}`, resolution 256, built from Lightformers: a ceiling strip (`light-0`, intensity 2, 0.3 x 60 m) along the centerline at y 4.9, two side panels (`light-1`, intensity 0.6), and one lobby panel. No HDRI files.
- **Fog:** `FogExp2`, `#0B0B0D`, density 0.045. The next case (12 m) is clearly visible; the one after (24 m) is a faint pool.
- **Tone mapping:** ACES Filmic, exposure 1.0, until WP-11. In WP-11 R2 captures the lobby, verafin and cfia stops under ACES Filmic and AgX side by side, picks one, and freezes it as the token `render.toneMapping` (`'aces' | 'agx'`, with `render.exposure`) in `src/design/tokens.ts`. Output color space sRGB.
- **Vignette:** CSS only, a fixed `pointer-events: none` layer over the canvas: `radial-gradient(ellipse at center, transparent 55%, rgba(11,11,13,0.55) 100%)`. No post-processing package.
- **Tiers:** high = DPR max 2, `MeshReflectorMaterial` floor, 3 spots, beam cones. Medium = DPR max 1.5, env-mapped floor, 1 spot, beam cones. Low = DPR 1, env-mapped floor, 1 spot, no beam cones. Only DPR adapts at runtime.

### 11.1 Six cheap delights
1. **The exhibition itself is a joke for engineers:** "Commit History", newest first, and the end wall that reads "You've reached the beginning."

### 4. Lighting recipe per tier

EXPERIENCE §7.5, §6.8 and §6.9; ARCHITECTURE §8.1 and §8.2. Values are tokens under `tokens.light` unless noted. The recipe is fixed per tier at boot; only DPR adapts at runtime.

**Ambient.** One hemisphere light: sky `#1A1C22` (`light.hemiSky`), ground `#0B0B0D` (`light.hemiGround`), intensity 0.15 (`light.hemiIntensity`).

**Real spotlights.**
- Count fixed at boot: **3 on high, 1 on medium and 1 on low.** They never change count at runtime.
- Angle 0.38 rad (about 21.8 degrees, `light.spotAngle`), penumbra 0.6 (`light.spotPenumbra`), decay 2 (`light.spotDecay`), `castShadow` false.
- Starting intensity **40 cd** (`light.spotIntensity`); R2 tunes it in WP-11 and freezes the token there (§9).
- Mounts (EXPERIENCE §5.5): standard plinths at (x_p, 4.6, z_p + 0.6); wing plinths at (sigma * 8.4, 3.7, z_p); mun at (-2.5, 4.6, -56.4); certifications at (4.4, 4.6, -57); desk at (0, 3.7, -66.9); portrait at (2.6, 5.4, -2.6). Each aims at its slot center (or target).
- Assignment on each arrival (EXPERIENCE §6.8): light 1 takes the current stop's target (or the next target ahead); on high, lights 2 and 3 take the next two targets ahead. A reassigned light moves instantly at intensity 0, takes the target's temperature color and fades up over 1 s (`motion.lightReassignS`). Hover or focus lifts the current stop's light to 1.35x (`motion.hoverLift`) over 250 ms; an open dialog dims the other real spots to 30% over 0.4 s.

**Zero shadow maps.** `gl.shadowMap.enabled = false`, no `castShadow` or `receiveShadow` anywhere.

**Baked pools and contact patches** (one floor `lightMap` CanvasTexture per zone, `environment/lightPools.ts`, R3):

| Element | Radius or size | Peak alpha or darkness | Color | Falloff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exhibit pool | 1.6 m (`light.poolRadius`), centered 0.3 m from the plinth toward the aisle center | 0.85 (`light.poolAlpha`) | target temperature (age ramp) | smoothstep |
| Door threshold pool | 1.2 m (`light.doorPoolRadius`), on the aisle floor | 0.35 (`light.doorPoolAlpha`) | `light-0` | smoothstep |
| Plinth contact patch | plinth footprint with a 0.25 m soft edge (`light.contactSoftEdge`) | darkness 0.55 (`light.contactDarkness`) | ink | smoothstep |
| Plinth-top contact shadow | drei `ContactShadows frames={1}`, rendered once after reveal | | | |
| Shadow decal (fallback for the line above, never both) | 0.6 m square, 64 px radial texture | opacity 0.45 | `--ink`, multiply | smoothstep |

Every target keeps its pool and wall wash whether or not a real light is assigned, so the far aisle always glows.

**Wall wash.** One shared 256 px "scallop" texture on the wall bay behind each target, tinted by the target temperature, with panel seams every 2 m baked in. It is the `wall` material's `lightMap` (§5).

**Environment.** drei `<Environment frames={1} resolution={256}>` built from Lightformers only: a ceiling strip (`light-0`, intensity 2, 0.3 x 60 m) along the centerline at y 4.9, two side panels (`light-1`, intensity 0.6) and one lobby panel. No HDRI files.

**Fog.** `FogExp2`, color `#0B0B0D`, density 0.045 (`light.fogDensity`). Fog factor is 1 - exp(-(0.045 d)^2): the next case at 12 m is about 25% fogged (clearly visible); the one after at 24 m is about 69% fogged (a faint pool).

For main work exhibit i of n, t = i / (n - 1) (0 when n = 1); spotlight, pool and wall-wash colors sample the ramp at t (stops at 0, 1/3, 2/3, 1, linear RGB interpolation). A wing uses its door: t = (k + 0.5) / (n - 1) for `after = w[k]`. Lobby and portrait use `light-0`; Foundations uses `light-3`; the desk uses `light-1`.
