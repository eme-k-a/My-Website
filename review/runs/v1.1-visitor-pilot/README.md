# First visit to the museum

Use the same preview for the whole session. Record its PR number, the phone model, browser, orientation and input method. Start at the entrance with the ordinary preview URL. Save debug mode and deep links for the separate performance check.

## Prompts for the visitor

Give one prompt at a time. Let the visitor choose their route. If they get stuck, record where they expected to go before helping.

1. Find the Round Table. Before opening its story, what does the arrangement suggest to you?
2. Show me the underside of the table, then look down on the laptops. Read the story and tell me whether it changes your interpretation.
3. Return to the place you left. Find the AWS credential and show me the reverse of its medal. Look at the whole cabinet again.
4. On the phone, start one vertical swipe on the medal and another on the text beside it. What moved each time? Did either gesture surprise you?
5. Get back to the gallery and find the résumé you would send to a colleague.

## Evidence so far

Emeka reported that rotating an artifact on his phone also scrolled the page. Device and browser details are pending. Retest on the phone that showed the problem. Record whether releasing a finger restores normal scrolling and whether rotating near the edge moves the surrounding page.

The rollout decision stays open until observations establish that visitors can read the controls, manipulate the artifact and return to the expected stop. Record the observed failure, its fix and the repeat visit before closing a failed task.
