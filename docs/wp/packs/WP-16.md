# WP-16: Quality tiers

## Goal
The gallery stays smooth and cool on phones: the quality tier is chosen once at boot and never switches light counts or materials at runtime (only DPR adapts), every shader program is compiled before it is needed (library materials at boot, exhibits before reveal), the canvas renders only while something moves and stops entirely 15 s after the last input, rendering pauses in hidden tabs and under the phone story sheet, and a lost WebGL context or a back/forward-cache return recovers by remounting the canvas, falling back to the classic resume after two losses.

## Notes
- Idle loops that never stop keep phones rendering at full rate until they throttle (within minutes). The 15 s idle stop and the demand frame loop are what make `frameloop="demand"` real.
- Mounting exhibits mid-glide compiles shaders and stalls phones 100 to 400 ms; precompile at boot and compile before reveal.
- Background tabs lose the WebGL context on iOS; CanvasTextures and troika glyph atlases can come back blank after in-place restore. Remount with a new key.
- Coming back from LinkedIn through the back/forward cache can show a dead canvas; `pageshow` with `persisted` must check `isContextLost()`.
- Low Power Mode caps rendering at 30 Hz; delta-time damping keeps motion correct.

## From ARCHITECTURE §8.4

### 8.4 Tier choice (boot time only, `quality/tiers.ts`)

`chooseTier({ coarse, cores, saveData, renderer, params })`, pure and unit-tested:
1. `?tier=low|medium|high` wins.
2. `?test=1` gives `'medium'`.
3. `saveData` (only reachable with an explicit museum choice) gives `'low'`.
4. Coarse pointer gives `'medium'`; `'low'` when `cores < 4` or the renderer matches the weak-GPU list (initially `/Mali-4|Mali-T|Adreno \(TM\) [345]\d\d|PowerVR SGX/i`). Safari has no `deviceMemory`, so it is not used.
5. Fine pointer: `'high'` when `cores >= 8` and the renderer does not match `/Intel|Mali|Adreno|SwiftShader|llvmpipe/i` (Apple GPUs qualify); otherwise `'medium'`.

The tier is stored once, written to `html[data-tier]`, and **never changes at runtime**: light count, floor material and antialias are fixed for the session. **Only DPR adapts**: start at `min(devicePixelRatio, maxDpr)` with `maxDpr` 2 / 1.5 / 1 for high / medium / low; drei `<PerformanceMonitor flipflops={3}>` lowers DPR in 0.25 steps to a floor of 1 and raises it back to `maxDpr`; `onFallback` locks DPR at 1. No AdaptiveDpr. `?test=1` fixes DPR at 1 and disables the monitor.

## From ARCHITECTURE §8.5

### 8.5 Mounting, compiling and frames

- Stop-graph distance: main stops by index; a wing item `k` is `doorIndex + k + 1` hops from the main track. Exhibits mount at distance <= 2 (hidden), compile with `gl.compileAsync`, then reveal; chunk preload at 3; unmount and dispose beyond 4.
- `frameloop="demand"` always. `FrameDriver` (R7) calls `invalidate()` every animation frame while the camera is moving, a fade or dolly runs, or a mount is compiling. While only idle motion runs, it invalidates every frame on fine pointers and on a 33 ms timer (30 fps) on coarse pointers. **Idle motion stops 15 s after the last input** (`scroll`, `pointermove`, `pointerdown`, `keydown`, all passive), so a settled, idle scene renders zero frames until the next input.
- Rendering pauses (no invalidation) when the story sheet covers 65% or more of the viewport (the phone sheet) and when `document.visibilityState === 'hidden'`.
- Dialog open on desktop: the camera dollies `tokens.camera.dialogDollyM` (0.6 m) toward the slot center over 0.9 s and back over 0.8 s on close (instant under reduced motion), and the other real spotlights dim to 30% (EXPERIENCE §6.5).

## From ARCHITECTURE §10.1

### 10.1 WebGL context loss (`quality/contextLoss.ts`, R7)

- `webglcontextlost`: `preventDefault()`, show the DOM veil "Restoring the gallery" (`context-veil`), increment the session loss count.
- `webglcontextrestored`: **remount** `<Canvas>` with a new `key` (`generation + 1`) instead of trusting three's in-place restore (CanvasTextures and troika atlases can come back blank).
- Fallback after **2 losses** in the session, or when a loss is not restored within 5 s: unmount the museum and `location.replace('/resume/#why-context-loss')` on `/` (§5.3); on an exhibit page switch `data-mode` to `classic` so `#story-static` shows.
- An error boundary around the canvas does the same fallback.

## From ARCHITECTURE §10.2

### 10.2 Back/forward cache

- `pageshow` with `event.persisted`: if `gl.isContextLost()`, remount (generation + 1); re-read `scrollY` into `progress.target`; `invalidate()`.
- No `unload` or `beforeunload` listeners, and no `Cache-Control: no-store` on HTML, so pages stay bfcache-eligible.

## From ARCHITECTURE §10.3

### 10.3 iOS Safari and touch

- Stage `height: 100lvh`, so the URL bar collapsing never resizes the drawing buffer. The stage height is applied through a CSS variable that `MuseumCanvas` updates only when the width changes or, on coarse pointers, when the height changes by 20% or more. Small height-only resizes are ignored.
- `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover">`; HUD and cards pad with `env(safe-area-inset-*)`. Page zoom is never disabled.
- `visibilitychange` to hidden: stop invalidating and pause the exhibit clock; on visible: resume and `invalidate()`.
- The dialog body uses `overscroll-behavior: contain`. No `touch-action: none` anywhere.
- DPR is clamped by tier (iPhones report 3).

### 10.4 Reduced motion

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (live listener): 200 ms fade between stops with no in-between camera poses (§6.5), no idle motion, no parallax, instant dialog dolly, `behavior: 'instant'` for every programmatic scroll.

### 13.3 Test hook (`src/app/testHooks.ts`, R4)

```ts
interface MuseumTestHook {
  ready: boolean;
  settled: boolean;
  stop: StopId;
  goTo(id: StopId, opts?: { instant?: boolean }): Promise<void>; // resolves when settled at id; switches tracks via the real enter/exit path, including the wing history entry (§6.7)
```

## From EXPERIENCE §6.7

### 6.7 Idle motion and frame loop
- Exhibits animate only while `runtime.motion` is true (§5.1).
- Idle motion stops 15 s after the last input (scroll, pointer, key or touch); the canvas then uses `frameloop="demand"` and renders nothing until input or a scripted move. On coarse pointers idle animation runs at 30 fps.
- Rendering pauses on `visibilitychange` (hidden) and while the phone sheet is open.
- `?test=1` freezes the clock, seeds the noise, disables idle motion (exhibits show their reduced-motion poses; `&idle=1` re-enables it for the idle-stop test only) and fixes the tier at medium (override with `&tier=high|medium|low`).

## From EXPERIENCE §7.5

### 7.5 Lighting recipe (fixed per tier at boot)
- **Ambient:** hemisphere light, sky `#1A1C22`, ground `#0B0B0D`, intensity 0.15.
- **Real spotlights:** 3 on high, 1 on medium and low (§6.8). Angle 0.38 rad, penumbra 0.6, decay 2, `castShadow` false. Starting intensity 40 cd, tuned by R2 in WP-11 and frozen as a token.
- **Zero shadow maps.**
- **Baked light pools:** `lightPools.ts` paints a CanvasTexture lightmap for each floor zone. Per target: a radial pool of radius 1.6 m, centered 0.3 m from the plinth toward the aisle center, peak alpha 0.85 in its temperature color, smoothstep falloff. Door thresholds get a smaller pool (radius 1.2 m, alpha 0.35, `light-0`) on the aisle floor so doors invite.
- **Contact shadows, rendered once:** painted into the same lightmap (plinth footprint, soft edge 0.25 m, darkness 0.55), plus the plinth-top contact shadow (§5.2).
- **Wall wash:** a shared 256 px "scallop" texture on wall bays behind each target, tinted by its temperature; panel seams every 2 m are baked into it.
- **Environment:** drei `Environment` with `frames={1}`, resolution 256, built from Lightformers: a ceiling strip (`light-0`, intensity 2, 0.3 x 60 m) along the centerline at y 4.9, two side panels (`light-1`, intensity 0.6), and one lobby panel. No HDRI files.
- **Fog:** `FogExp2`, `#0B0B0D`, density 0.045. The next case (12 m) is clearly visible; the one after (24 m) is a faint pool.
- **Tone mapping:** ACES Filmic, exposure 1.0, until WP-11. In WP-11 R2 captures the lobby, verafin and cfia stops under ACES Filmic and AgX side by side, picks one, and freezes it as the token `render.toneMapping` (`'aces' | 'agx'`, with `render.exposure`) in `src/design/tokens.ts`. Output color space sRGB.
- **Vignette:** CSS only, a fixed `pointer-events: none` layer over the canvas: `radial-gradient(ellipse at center, transparent 55%, rgba(11,11,13,0.55) 100%)`. No post-processing package.
- **Tiers:** high = DPR max 2, `MeshReflectorMaterial` floor, 3 spots, beam cones. Medium = DPR max 1.5, env-mapped floor, 1 spot, beam cones. Low = DPR 1, env-mapped floor, 1 spot, no beam cones. Only DPR adapts at runtime.

## From EXPERIENCE §8.2

### 8.2 Loading states
1. **First paint:** hero and every section card are static HTML. The page already scrolls and snaps; the cards work without the canvas.
2. **Museum loading:** a reserved 20 px row under the hero links shows a brass hairline that draws across with "Lights coming up 64%" (`role="progressbar"`). The row is in the prerendered HTML, so nothing shifts.
3. **Ready** (everything mounted at that moment has resolved Suspense, compiled and synced troika text, then 2 frames rendered): the canvas fades in over 600 ms at the camera pose for the current scroll position, and the loading row fades out.
4. **Later exhibits** mount 2 stops ahead. If one is not ready at arrival, the case shows the crate (§5.7) and the card adds "Installation in progress".
5. **Context loss:** a veil reads "Restoring the gallery"; the canvas remounts with a new key. After 2 losses in the session, or no restore within 5 s, the gallery falls back to classic mode (§8.5): `/` goes to `/resume/#why-context-loss`, and an exhibit page shows its prerendered story.
