# F-039: on phones the wing HUD covers the wall title at the wing stops

## Goal
Close P1 F-039 from G3 review round 1 (`review/runs/2026-10-21-g3r1/scorecard.md`, `review/findings.json`). This is the last item blocking gate G3; the round 1 average is already 3.78.

On portrait phones, the wing head ("Return to the aisle" button, x 16 to 189, y 96 to 140 on Pixel 7) is drawn over the 3D wall title at the wing exhibit stops:
- **Pixel 7 (412 x 839):** finance-tracker; the V of sdsj's V1 touches the control.
- **360 x 640:** klarup, finance-tracker and sdsj.

The cause is that F-M3 raised the wing titles (F-027), but portrait framing does not reserve space for the wing head. Evidence:
- the `probe-*-small.png` captures and `probe-wing-hud.mjs` in that folder;
- the committed CI baseline `stop-finance-tracker-pixel7.png`.

## Owner role
R4 Camera with R6 Overlay. There is no context pack: read this brief, the round 3 G2 F-020 fix (`review/reports/F-G2r2.json`: framing from the measured card top), the F-038 fix, and the end of `docs/RULINGS.md`.

## Fix
At every wing exhibit stop on Pixel 7 and at 360 x 640, no wing HUD rect may intersect the projected wall title. That covers the chip, the exit control and the rail label, against the numeral and organization lines of the title.

Two acceptable approaches:
- **Preferred (R4):** on portrait, frame wing stops from the measured bottom of the wing head, as F-020 frames the desk from the measured card top. Keep the title top at least 8 px below it.
- **Alternative (R6):** a compact wing head on phones.

Do not regress:
- desktop;
- F-020 (desk), F-036 (wing rail label), F-037 (lone wing case) and F-038 (door on the wing track);
- the case framing at the main exhibits.

## Acceptance
- Extend `tests/e2e/rail-input.spec.ts` (g) "at each wing stop" to pixel7 and to a 360 x 640 portrait context. With the fix disabled, the new check must fail.
- The `probe-wing-hud.mjs` method (or the spec) reports 0 overlapping pixels at all six rows: 3 wing stops at 2 sizes.
- `npm run verify` and `npm run build` pass, as do the camera, rail-input, wings, dialog and keyboard specs on desktop and pixel7.
- Re-take the captures at the three wing stops on pixel7 and at 360 x 640, and look at them.
