- A3. Transfer. Proof: `transfer.spec.ts`. Threshold: at most 1.2 MB and 4 MB.
- A4. Vitals. Proof: `vitals.spec.ts`. Threshold: LCP element `h1`; CLS at most 0.02.
- A5. Lighthouse scores the museum. Proof: `lh-precheck.spec.ts`. Threshold: `museum` and `ready`.
- A6. Lighthouse gates (local advisory, CI authoritative). Proof: `npm run lh` locally and the CI `lighthouse` job after merge (`gh run list --workflow ci.yml --limit 1 --json jobs`). Threshold: all error-level assertions pass; the job is blocking and green.

- The mobile 30 fps floor cannot be measured in SwiftShader; it is Emeka's manual G5 check (WP-32 checklist).
