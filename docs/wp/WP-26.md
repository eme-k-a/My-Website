# WP-26: Lighting and atmosphere

## Goal
The gallery's mood reaches its final quality: a tuned light recipe per stop and tier, light and placard paper that visibly warm with age down the aisle, a CSS vignette, polished beams, fog and wall washes, and the portrait's duotone-to-color warmth on hover. Budgets stay green, and the review average reaches gate G4's 4.0.

## Owner role
R3 Environment Builder with R2 Art Director (R2 tunes and freezes light token values and signs off).

## Milestone and dependencies
M4, after gate G3. Must be merged first: WP-20 to WP-25. Parallel with: WP-27, WP-28. Feeds: G4 (review average at least 4.0, no dimension below 3.5; Emeka signs off 10 screenshots).
