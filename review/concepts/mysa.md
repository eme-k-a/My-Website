## Reference (from EXPERIENCE §4.2)

- **Materials:** ceramic, walnut, brass, accentGlow.
- **Motion (one):** the dial segments light in order, one every 2 s (each ramps 0.15 to 1.6 emissive over 0.6 s), all four hold lit until 8.5 s, then fade together to rest over 1 s. Period 10 s. Key, ring and slab are static.
- **Reduced-motion pose:** all four segments lit at intensity 1.0.
- **Object description (DOM):** "A ring dial on a wall unit lights four segments in turn (build, end-to-end tests, dev release, store release) as the release pipeline warms up. At its foot, a brass key passes through a ring: the single sign-on token exchange."

## Checklist (verbatim from EXPERIENCE §4.2)

- [x] Reads as one object: an upright slab with a ring dial, with the key and ring at its foot.
- [x] The slab is generic: rounded rectangle, no screen, no buttons, no logo, not a copy of a real product.
- [x] Exactly four dial segments; the only motion is their sequential lighting on a 10 s loop.
- [x] The key visibly passes through the ring (they overlap); neither moves.
- [x] No phone silhouettes, no orbiting parts, no text.
- [x] Inside the standard slot; ≤ 20k triangles.
- [x] Only ceramic, walnut, brass, accentGlow.
- [x] Reduced-motion pose shows all four segments lit.

## Evidence (one line per box, same order)

Build `e57fb74` (WP-20), `?test=1&mode=museum`, captured with the `@capture` spec into `out/wp20/` (scratch, regenerated by the WP-20 commands); motion probes under `&idle=1` into `out/wp20/motion/`.

1. Thumbnail `out/wp20/mysa-thumb.png` (and `out/wp20/high/mysa-thumb.png`): one silhouette on the walnut tray, a tall light slab with the teal ring dial on its face, the small brass ring and key at its right foot; strong value contrast against the case and wall. The ring and key read as a ring with a key through it only after the 35 degree turn of that assembly (see Notes).
2. `dial.ts` `roundedSlab`: a plain rounded rectangle (corner radius 0.03, rounded edges); the only parts on its face are the dial and its bezel. No screen plane, no buttons, no logo, no texture in `WarmStart.tsx`.
3. Four `segment-i` meshes (`SEGMENTS`, unit-tested 80 degree arcs at 45, 135, 225 and 315 degrees clockwise from the top); only their `emissiveIntensity` changes (`segmentGlow`: 0.5 + 2i s starts, 0.6 s ramps, hold to 8.5 s, 1 s fade, 10 s period, tested). With `&idle=1` the canvas changes (`out/wp20/motion/mysa-moved-*.png`), manifest `motion: 'slow'`.
4. `dial.test.ts` "passes the key through the ring": the shaft runs through the ring center from the bow side to the teeth side and clears the tube; nothing on the key, ring or stand is written after the first layout (`out/wp20/mysa-desktop.png` shows the shaft through the ring).
5. No phone shape, no orbiting part (nothing but the dial levels changes), no Text, Html or texture in the component; the shots show no text on the object.
6. `stats().exhibits.mysa` on desktop and pixel7: `inBounds: true`, 4,820 triangles (budget 6,000, limit 20,000), 12 draw calls; `exhibit-contract.spec.ts` passes on both.
7. `WarmStart.tsx` uses `useMaterial` for walnut, ceramic and brass and four `useMaterialInstance('accentGlow')` clones; nothing else.
8. `dialGlow(t, true)` is `[1, 1, 1, 1]` for every t (tested); reduced motion with `&idle=1` keeps the canvas byte-identical over 12 s (pixel7 probe), and the `?test=1` shots (`out/wp20/mysa-desktop.png`) show all four segments lit evenly.
