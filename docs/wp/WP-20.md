## Forbidden
- Standard list (`docs/wp/_TEMPLATE.md`); everything ESLint bans in exhibit folders (ARCHITECTURE §7.5); cross-exhibit imports.
- Mysa: phone silhouettes, an orbiting key, a screen, buttons or anything copying a real thermostat; Wailo: chat bubbles, copied app UI, readable text, logos.
- A second motion, more than 20,000 triangles each, leaving the slot.

## Acceptance
For each of `mysa` and `wailo` (the WP-12 criteria):
- A1. `npm run verify` and the registry test pass.
- A2. Exhibit chunk at most 40 KB gzip (`npm run check:budgets`).
- A3. `exhibit-contract.spec.ts`: `inBounds === true`, `triangles <= 20000`, click at `screenPos(id)` opens the dialog with the artwork title, Esc returns focus to `open-<id>`, frames identical 3 s apart under `?test=1`; on desktop and pixel7.
- A4. R2 checklist: 0 unchecked boxes and Verdict `PASS` in `review/concepts/<id>.md`.
- A5. Wailo arc peak at or below local y 0.675 (inside the slot), proven by `inBounds`.

## Notes
- Keep the two objects visibly from the same hand as Verafin: same tray thickness, same brass and walnut, the same restraint (Verafin approved at G2 is the reference).
- One motion each, driven by `useExhibitClock()`; per-mesh emissive animation uses `useMaterialInstance('accentGlow')`.
- The Mysa slab is deliberately generic: no screen, no buttons, no logo.
- The Wailo tag is blank: "App Store release" lives in the DOM object description, never in 3D.
- R0 ruling (2026-10-15): the Wailo reset-bar flash uses ramps of at least 0.5 s, per ART_DIRECTION §5 glow levels and EXPERIENCE §10.4. That makes it 0.4 to 1.6 to 0.4 over at least 1.0 s, not 0.8 s.
