# WP-19: Slice review

## Goal
An independent, evidence-based score of the vertical slice against the art-direction rubric, plus a findings list with severities, so gate G2 can decide whether the style is right before four builders fan out. This round's approved stills become the reference for every later review.

## Owner role
R11 Experience Reviewer. Never edits code.

## Milestone and dependencies
M2. Must be merged first: WP-17 (slice tests and the `@capture` spec green) and, if available, WP-18 (preview URL). Parallel with: none. Feeds: G2 (average at least 3.5, no open P0 or P1, Emeka's approval).

## Inputs
- `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md` §7 (rubric D1 to D6 with anchors), §8 (concept checklist process), §10 (reference stills, empty until now).
- PLAN §6 (continuous improvement loop: severities, stop rules, cap of 3 rounds per milestone).
- EXPERIENCE §1 (principles), §4.1 and §4.7 (the two built exhibits), §7, §8.1 (fast path), §11 (delights and do-nots).
- `review/concepts/verafin.md` and `review/concepts/safe-sip.md` (R2 verdicts).
- The preview URL from `review/reports/WP-18.json` when it exists.

## Owned globs (exclusive)
- `review/rubric.md`, `review/findings.json`, `review/runs/**` (scorecards and JSON; PNGs are gitignored)
- `review/reports/WP-19.json`

## Forbidden
- Standard list (`docs/wp/_TEMPLATE.md`).
- Editing any code, test, spec, doc outside `review/` (other than your report), or the concept checklists (R2's).
- Scores without a cited screenshot path; absolute praise or blame without comparison to the anchors.
- Committing PNGs.

## Acceptance
- A1. Complete scorecard. Proof: `review/runs/<date>-g2/scorecard.md`. Threshold: a score for every (slice stop x D1 to D6 x desktop and Pixel 7), each with a screenshot path; averages computed.
- A2. Valid findings. Proof: the `node -e` findings check. Threshold: `findings ok`; P0 and P1 counts stated in the scorecard.
- A3. G2 inputs ready. Proof: scorecard sections. Threshold: overall average reported against the 3.5 bar, open P0/P1 list, proposed reference stills listed.

## Output
- Commits: `docs(review): slice review round 1 scorecard and findings`. Ends with `WP: WP-19` and the attribution lines.
- `review/reports/WP-19.json` with the overall average and P0/P1 counts in `notes`.

## Size
S. One capture run and a careful written review.

## Notes
- SwiftShader at tier medium is not what most desktop visitors see; score desktop at `tier=high`, and judge mood relative to the anchors, not in absolute terms.
- A model score cannot stand in for Emeka's taste: G2 also requires his explicit approval of the stills and the preview link. Make the stills easy to approve (named files, one line each).
- Busy objects drift toward "programmer art"; D6 (one object in a 320 px grayscale thumbnail) is the most direct check of that.
- Cap: 3 review rounds per milestone; a finding reopened twice becomes a taste call for Emeka.
